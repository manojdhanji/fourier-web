About Fourier Visualization

What This Demo Shows

This visualization demonstrates how a complex shape can be reconstructed using a chain of rotating vectors. Each vector corresponds to a single Fourier coefficient, and together they trace out the curve over time.

How It Works

  • The shape is sampled as a sequence of complex numbers.
  • A Discrete Fourier Transform extracts frequency components.
  • Each component becomes a rotating vector with: magnitude, phase, and angular velocity.
  • The vectors are linked head‑to‑tail to form an epicycle chain.
  • The endpoint of the final vector traces the reconstructed shape.

What You Can Do

Use the controls to:

  1. Select a predefined shape or generate a random one.
  2. Start, pause, or reset the animation.
  3. Zoom in and out to explore the vector chain.
  4. Clear the trace to observe the motion more clearly.

This demo is powered by a lightweight JavaScript engine that computes and animates Fourier epicycles in real time.
